Tribal Kingdom of Fish City

Fish City, officially the Tribal Kingdom of Fish City, is a country in the Western Pacific Ocean. It is comprised of 8 mainland provinces, as well as 5 non-mainland provinces and 4 territories. Its 8 mainland provinces stretch from Prince of Wales Island in southern Alaska to the 170 degree west longitude line in the Pacific Ocean and to southern Chile, making it the world's largest country with over 10 million square miles of territory. The country's northern provinces of Hallowed Sea, Frogville, Fish Village, and Derathon are the center of most of its population, with Frogville being the most populous province and having the most populated city. Komodo City in Derathon is the nation's capital, and it is also the 3rd largest city after the cities of Sheperton and Ostabohn.
