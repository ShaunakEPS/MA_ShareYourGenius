Angus Bagpipes

Angus Loreau Bagpipes is a Fishish politician and economist who has served as the 20th prime minister of Fish City since 2021. He has served as leader of the liberal party and MP for Braurveau since 2021, and he previously served as Prime Minister of Bearview from 1998 to 2002 and President of Fishlantic City from 2004 to 2012.

Bagpipes was born in Ozrkaun in 1969, a small town located in Bearview's Nalzron Okrug. He was raised here, and he got his bachelor's degree in Economics at the Royal University of Glenview in 1990, and he received a master's degree here in 1994. After his tenure as prime minister of Bearview, Angus Bagpipes received his doctorate in economics at Starfishvue University in 2004. He was able to meet the requirements in two years for a doctorate because of what he described as "sleepless nights, foodless days."
